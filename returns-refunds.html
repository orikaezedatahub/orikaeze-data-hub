Returns & Refund Policy

Your satisfaction matters to us.

At ORIKAEZE HUB, customer satisfaction matters to us. We want every customer to receive exactly what they ordered and have a smooth shopping experience.

1. Wrong Product

If you receive a different product from what you ordered, contact us promptly. We will investigate and arrange an appropriate solution.

2. Damaged Products

If your product arrives damaged, notify us as soon as possible and provide clear pictures or videos showing the issue.

3. Missing Items

If an item is missing from your order, contact us so we can investigate and resolve the issue.

4. Returns

Returns may be accepted where a product is damaged, defective or incorrectly supplied. The issue should be reported promptly, and the product should be kept in its original condition where possible.

5. Refunds

Refunds may be issued where:

  • An order cannot be fulfilled after payment.
  • A confirmed product issue cannot reasonably be resolved through replacement or correction.
  • An incorrect or missing item cannot be supplied.

Approved refunds will be processed through an agreed payment method. We will communicate the expected processing time.

6. Change of Mind

For correctly supplied products, returns or refunds are not automatic simply because a customer changes their mind. Any applicable consumer rights will still be respected.

7. How to Report an Issue

Contact us through WhatsApp at +234 904 868 3395. Please provide your order details and any relevant pictures or videos to help us resolve the issue.
